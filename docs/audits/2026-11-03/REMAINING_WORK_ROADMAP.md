This is a proposed development/acceptance plan. Nothing below has been implemented, activated, purchased or scheduled by the audit.

## Scope and estimate

**Shortest practical route: one bounded customer-release change set, existing recovery tools, and one combined acceptance programme.** Reuse the present PostgreSQL, R2, Web, execution/workflow dispatchers, Native Worker, ledger, review and Xendit adapters. No replacement engine, new GPU, new storage platform or long infrastructure programme is justified by this audit.

One developer workday means focused effort by an experienced developer who understands this code, including normal verification/debugging. The ranges are a bottom-up planning estimate, not measured implementation time or promised calendar dates. Some tasks can overlap; the totals are **person-days**, not parallel elapsed days. Merchant onboarding, channel certification, legal/owner review, domain/DNS decisions and waiting for creative decisions are excluded external elapsed time. New architectural requirements or major failures found during acceptance require a revised estimate.

The beta estimate includes ordinary fixes exposed by the described checks, not an assumption that the current gates can simply be switched on. Generalization of the fixed pilot, real test/production payment separation and the original multi-output acceptance are explicit work.

### What to do immediately next

Prepare the concrete B1/B2 change set for review, and B5's existing-tool recovery configuration, without running paid work. Resolve the Workspace-wallet versus account-wallet decision before code changes obscure the contractual difference. The owner can inspect the existing [official clip](https://ai-test.proyaofficial.com/content/552f4011-8354-4e61-aef5-79519003d46d) and its pending review separately; this audit makes no creative decision.

Avoid separate releases for Script, speech, one Video, one clip, each replay case and each individual original owner step. The existing real 1x1x1 pilot has already established those technical outputs. The missing evidence is a complete ordinary-customer journey and its failure/permission/payment behavior.

### Paid acceptance size and funds

At the retained 20-second example prices, a 2x2x2 plan with two TTS recordings is **14,150 Tokens before replacements**. It creates two Script Jobs, two TTS Jobs, four Video Jobs and four Clipper Jobs producing eight planned clips. This exceeds ten Jobs, so the browser-closure acceptance can be combined with the graph test. The existing 5,385 balance is insufficient by 8,765 Tokens; that is a planning calculation, not a persisted quote or a funding instruction.

Use the test-payment path for test funds and the authorized Live purchase for real paid admission. Freeze a new complete quote at execution time. Quote and authorize any rejection/regeneration or paid retry separately. The pilot's $6.4902 historical bill is not the budget for four or more new Videos, and its deterministic zero-provider Clipper completion does not price ordinary automatic analysis.

Keep general 20-item batches disabled in the first beta if they have not passed their separate acceptance. That defers Stage 5 breadth while preserving the original required small Workflow. Do not describe the beta as full original-PDF parity until those deferred checks pass.

## Reference to the matrix

The CSV `Roadmap_task` column maps each requirement to B1-B9, P1/P2, O1/O2, or MAINTAIN. MAINTAIN means preserve the bounded verified behavior; it does not mean its surrounding page or complete customer journey has passed. Severity is independent of task ID: public-operations task P2 still has P1 priority.

For literal full-PDF parity, close every matrix row or obtain a clearly documented product-scope amendment. The default paid/public estimates assume the current shared billing-account model is explicitly accepted. They do not assume the 125 historical payloads can be recovered by development work; recovery needs an authentic surviving source. Honest loss handling and prevention are required even when the original bytes cannot be restored.
